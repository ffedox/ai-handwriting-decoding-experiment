# Original Prompt

**Status:** Original text used in the experiment  
**Purpose:** Convert handwritten SEO notes into an article  
**Input:** Images of handwritten notes  
**Models tested:** Claude Opus, Gemini, and others  
**Date tested:** 2026-08-16

> The prompt below is reproduced exactly as used in the experiment.
> No wording has been edited for clarity or corrected after the test.

---

I am providing images of my handwritten notes taken from a video on how to do SEO in 2026. Please turn these notes into an article by following these strict instructions:

1. Strict Source Limitation: Your output must be based EXCLUSIVELY on the content visible in the photos provided. Do not add any of your own knowledge, external facts, or general information about SEO. If it is not explicitly written in my notes, do not include it.

2. Exact Ordering: Process and write the content in the exact same order as it appears in the notes. Use the filenames of the images to determine the correct chronological order. Do not rearrange, group, or skip anything.

3. Direct Interpretation: Interpret exactly what you see in the images (including expanding my abbreviations based on the context of the notes) and turn it directly into the body of the text.

4. Structure & Title:
* Title: Create a title conceptually similar to "Comparing results of written notes by hand and turning them into a written summary of a video vs just summarizing the transcription of the video results". However, you must apply the specific style advice for writing blog post titles that is mentioned within my notes/the video.
* Introduction: Write the first part of the article as a dedicated introduction.
* The Guide: Following the intro, provide the full summary of my notes formatted as a guide, strictly adhering to the chronological order and source limitations mentioned above.

---

## Notes on usage

- The same prompt was used for each model.
- The image files were supplied in the same order.
- The filenames were used to establish chronological order.
